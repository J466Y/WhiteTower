# Code of conduct

White Tower adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and events) and to anyone representing the project.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, accept constructive criticism gracefully, focus on what is best for the community, and do not harass, insult or demean anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). A dedicated private contact address will be published here before the first public release; until then, reach a maintainer through the contact options on their GitHub profile.

Every report is reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in a report take no part in handling it. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the severity.
